# Code of Conduct

We want Glori to be a welcoming project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact options.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
